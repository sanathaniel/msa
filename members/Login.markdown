<p>We contract with Johns Hopkins University Press (JHUP) for publishing and membership management services. To join MSA, first <a href="https://www.press.jhu.edu/journals/cart/register">create an account</a>, then <a href="https://www.press.jhu.edu/journals/cart/for-sale?oc=42">purchase your membership here.</a> Returning members may <a href="https://www.press.jhu.edu/journals/cart/log-in">log-in</a> here. Members also may join the <a href="https://groups.google.com/g/msa-members-list">MSA Members Listserv</a>, where we share announcements.
</p>

<p>Refer to the <a href="https://www.press.jhu.edu/about/jhup-privacy-policy">JHUP Privacy Policy</a> for details on use and protection of your account data.</p>
